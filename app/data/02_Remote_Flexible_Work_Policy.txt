PURPOSE, SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

The Remote and Flexible Work Policy defines when employees may work away from a company office and the
controls that apply. It covers hybrid work, temporary remote work, and approved fully remote roles.

Hybrid employee: a role assigned to a company office that may work remotely on approved days. Remote
employee: a role whose primary work location is outside a company office. Temporary remote work: a time-
limited arrangement for a specific personal or operational reason.

The standard hybrid model is three office days and up to two remote days each workweek unless the
business unit publishes a different approved pattern. Team leaders may designate anchor days for
collaboration.

ELIGIBILITY AND APPROVAL

Eligibility depends on job duties, performance, data-access requirements, customer commitments, and the
employee's ability to work safely and productively away from the office.

Employees under a formal performance improvement plan are not automatically excluded, but remote-work
requests may be limited when closer coaching or onsite supervision is necessary.

A manager may approve temporary remote work for up to 10 working days. Longer arrangements require People
Operations approval and, where relevant, Information Security and Tax review.

WORKING HOURS, AVAILABILITY AND COMMUNICATION

Employees must remain available during team core hours of 11:00 to 16:00 local time unless the role uses
shifts or another documented schedule. Flexible start and end times are permitted when coverage and total
working-hour requirements are met.

Remote work does not change meeting, response-time, or performance expectations. Employees should keep
calendar status current and use approved communication channels.

Overtime, weekend work, and time-off-in-lieu rules remain unchanged. Employees must not work excessive
hours merely because they are remote.

SECURITY, PRIVACY AND EQUIPMENT

Company-managed devices must be used for confidential work unless an exception is documented. Multi-
factor authentication, screen lock, endpoint protection, and approved VPN controls must remain enabled.

Employees must avoid discussing confidential information where others can overhear it and must not leave
sensitive papers unattended in public or shared spaces.

The company may provide a laptop, headset, security key, and one standard external monitor. Loss, theft,
or suspected compromise must be reported to the service desk and Information Security immediately.

TRAVEL, CROSS-BORDER WORK AND EXPENSES

Remote work from another country is not automatically permitted. Any cross-border work request of more
than five working days must be reviewed in advance for tax, immigration, payroll, employment-law,
insurance, and information-security implications.

Normal commuting costs are not reimbursable. Pre-approved business travel from the assigned work location
is reimbursed under the Travel and Expense Policy.

Employees may claim up to INR 1,500 per month for approved home internet costs when their employment
letter or remote-work addendum explicitly provides the allowance.

PERFORMANCE, EXCEPTIONS AND FAQ

Performance is assessed using role outcomes, quality, collaboration, customer impact, reliability, and
agreed goals rather than physical visibility.

A remote-work arrangement may be changed or withdrawn when business needs, security requirements,
performance concerns, or regulatory considerations require it. Managers should provide reasonable notice
except in urgent cases.

FAQ: Can I work from a cafe? Low-risk work may be performed from a cafe if privacy can be maintained;
confidential calls and sensitive records should not be handled in open public areas. Can I permanently
move cities? A location change requires written approval because compensation, tax, office assignment,
and employment terms may be affected.
